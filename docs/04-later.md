Things we want flowd to do but have not built yet. Each one is planned

## Weekly and monthly review

Look back over a week or a month to hold yourself accountable: how much
you focused, how often you were pulled away and how much you finished.
A first design with stats, a timeline, a week chart and a month heatmap
was tried and set aside because it felt busy. Start again from the few
numbers that help you decide what to change next week.
