# Later

What flowd should do next but does not yet. Each idea is planned before any code is written, and
taken off this list once it is built.

## Weekly and monthly review

Look back over a week or a month to hold yourself accountable: how much you focused, how often you
were pulled away and how much you finished.

A first design, with stats, a timeline, a week chart and a month heatmap, was tried and set aside
because it felt busy. Start again from the few numbers that would change how you plan the next
week, and keep the screen as calm as the rest of flowd.
